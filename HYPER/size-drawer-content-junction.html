Find Your Size

Hyperlite Mountain Gear uses your torso length to match you with the right fit for our backpacks. Check out our video instructions here or follow the steps below.

01

Find your C7 vertebra

Tilt your chin to your chest. The most prominent bony bump at the base of your neck is your C7 vertebra. Have a friend mark it.

02

Find your iliac crest

Stand upright. Place both hands on your hips, thumbs pointing back, and slide down to the top shelf of your hip bones. The imaginary line between your thumbs is the bottom of your measurement.

↕︎ Height and torso length don't reliably match. Always measure — don't estimate from height.
03

Measure along your spine

Have a friend run a flexible tape from your C7 down along the curve of your spine to the iliac crest line. That distance is your torso length.
