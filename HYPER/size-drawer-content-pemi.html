Fit Overview

The Pemi 15 is sized by chest circumference, not torso length. A snug, wrap-around fit is essential to limit bounce while running. Because the vest has minimal stretch (aside from the front shock cord), finding the right size depends on your build:

  • Athletic/Alpine Build: If you have a larger chest that tapers toward a narrower waist, erring toward the smaller size for your chest measurement may result in a more comfortable fit.
  • Straight Build: If there is less discrepancy between your chest and waist, the larger size option may be better.
  • Preference: If you prefer a relaxed fit or plan to wear more layers, we recommend sizing up.

How to Measure
  1. Widest Point: Measure around the fullest part of your chest.
  2. Wrap it Around: Keep tape level under armpits and across shoulder blades.
  3. Check the Fit: Take measurement while breathing normally.
  4. Layer Up: Measure while wearing the layers you intend to run in.
