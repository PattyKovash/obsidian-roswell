Critical — customer-facing regressions

6 items

These change what a shopper sees or what data gets sent. Confirm and re-implement before launch.

layout/theme.liquid
missing

Two things dropped from the document head/body: the web_checkout cart-attribute fetch script, and the {% render 'beam-imports' %} call between the BEAM START/END markers. The Beam snippet files themselves still exist in the new theme — they're just no longer included anywhere, so Beam won't load at all until this render call is re-added.

blocks/_product-details.liquid
missing

The entire live "recently viewed" view-counter script is gone — the fetches to qa.wpweb.co.in/social-threads/view-counter.php, the session tracking, the decrement-on-unload beacon. Note it's pointed at a qa. host with debug console.log calls left in, worth cleaning up on re-add.

blocks/swatches.liquid
missing

The "N RECENTLY VIEWED" badge driven by the metafields.custom.view_count value is gone — this is the display half of the view-counter feature above.

blocks/_product-card-gallery.liquid
missing

The {% render 'product-card-badges' %} call was removed along with a wrapper-div comment-out. The badge snippet exists in the new theme, but nothing on the product-card-gallery path calls it anymore.

snippets/cart-summary.liquid
missing

The {% render 'beam-select-nonprofit' %} call (BEAM START/END block) is gone from the cart summary — the nonprofit picker won't show in cart even though Beam is otherwise wired up.
